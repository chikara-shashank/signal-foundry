# Frozen research protocol — 25 September 2026

Written before running the new comparisons. These are retrospective tests, not a prospective holdout or a reproduction of authors' leveraged returns. Earlier studies in this repository already inspected much of this history.

- Data: existing Alpaca SIP SPY and QQQ minute-bar archive, January 2016 through the last complete cached session. Verify calendar coverage, OHLC validity and unique contiguous timestamps; exclude and count incomplete sessions. Preserve the archive; hash input files.
- Candidates, with no parameter search: existing long-only noise-area rule; repaired noise-area rule requiring price above both the upper band and session VWAP; long-only one-minute session VWAP trend from Zarattini/Aziz. Long-only is an adaptation to the engine. VWAP uses minute typical price `(high + low + close) / 3`, consistently in replay and new production rules.
- Noise decisions: every 30 minutes from 10:00 to 30 minutes before calendar close; 14 preceding sessions, at least 10 observations at each mark. No entry using a stale or missing completed minute. VWAP trend: completed minutes from 09:31; exit below VWAP. No new entry in the last 10 minutes. The replay exits five minutes before close, matching the engine's session protection.
- Execution: act at the next minute open after the decision, then separately stress one further minute of delay. Never fill at the close that generated the signal. One position per symbol, integer shares; $10,000 engine capital, $500 maximum position and $3,000 gross cap. A 150-bp emergency stop and 5% protective target are explicit adaptations; ambiguous bars take the stop first, gaps through stops pay the open. No leverage, shorts or overnight exposure.
- Cost scenarios per side: half spread 0.13 bps SPY / 0.14 QQQ (prior journal estimates, not historical quotes), plus 1 bp slippage for commission-free sensitivity; engine scenario adds 3 bps slippage and 1 bp fee; adverse scenario 6 bps slippage and 1 bp fee. Zero commission does not remove spread or slippage. Unused cash earns zero.
- Report full period, 2016–2023 development window, 2024–2025 retrospective validation and 2026 final evaluation separately; disclose all comparisons, rejected hypotheses, trade counts, win rate, net dollars, drawdown and daily returns. Diagnostic fixed-notional results must not be confused with engine-sized returns. Use daily-block bootstrap confidence intervals, not independent-trade significance.
- Options: examine volatility-risk-premium literature and add a defined-risk bearish call-credit companion to the existing put-credit experiment. The 21–45 DTE, 0.20–0.35 delta, IV/realized-volatility >=1.25, width/risk/cost limits are frozen engineering adaptations, not parameters established by the papers. No naked positions. Both credit directions share the existing $200 aggregate risk limit and $100 per-spread cap.
- Options P/L requires time-stamped OPRA bid/ask on both legs, contract metadata/OI available at the decision, later quotes after latency, and intact observation history. Historical trade candles or present-day Greeks must not be substituted. If these are unavailable, record a blocked result, test accounting and replay determinism with explicitly synthetic fixtures, and retain the new hypothesis disabled. Never report fixture P/L as investment evidence.
- Promotion: no strategy is automatically enabled. All remain unvalidated until fresh, executable-price portfolio evidence survives costs and independent review. No changes to the active $2,000 engine daily-loss setting.

Implementation clarification after the first diagnostic run: carry remaining cash between sessions, cap stop risk at the existing default $10, enforce the existing 50-bp entry-price displacement check, and retain the $2,000 daily loss gate. These accounting constraints were missing from the first replay implementation; no signal parameters were changed in response to returns. The primary next-bar-open fill is an optimistic zero-delay approximation at the decision boundary; the adverse scenario delays it by another minute. This is an isolated strategy study, not a complete multi-strategy engine/portfolio validation.
