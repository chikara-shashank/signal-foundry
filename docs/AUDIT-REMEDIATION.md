# Audit remediation — v1.10.0

The active stock paper engine's **$2,000 daily-loss override is retained**. No strategy has earned a live qualification. Fixing execution and research infrastructure is not evidence of positive expectancy.

## Implemented boundaries

- Broker requests share an in-process credential/host budget. Background activity/options reads stop at 90 requests per rolling minute, entries at 120, protection reads at 170 and protection writes at 180. A 429 backs off without retrying order mutations. Stable parent orders refresh every 30 seconds; quantity changes, notifications and missing stops force a refresh. This preserves headroom; it cannot promise execution during a provider outage.
- Crypto exit triggers are persisted by symbol and entry before waiting for the coordinator. Two simultaneous stops remain distinct. Bracket overfills, replacement orders and owned-quantity conflicts produce durable incidents. Their P/L is unavailable. Ordinary flatten does not guess ownership or close an external short. Incident recovery still requires a broker/journal review.
- Shared accounts allow unrelated external short holdings. An externally reserved QQQ underlying stops the noise strategy from reserving idle capital. The dashboard displays the noise strategy's fixed-notional exception and nominal stop risk separately from ordinary per-trade limits; a nominal stop is not a guaranteed maximum loss.
- New entry orders retain a strategy manifest: source hash, signal/risk settings, fees, enabled portfolio, account policy and experiment ID. Results can be filtered by experiment and entry dates (UTC, exclusive end). Legacy history stays separate. All stock live entries are blocked pending independent qualification; paper and existing exit management remain available.
- Broker activities are imported idempotently with a durable cursor. Order-linked fees are provisional, fill discrepancies and option lifecycle events are visible, and economic reporting subtracts recorded/reserved model charges plus optional `OPERATING_COST_PER_DAY_USD`. This does **not** certify a full broker statement reconciliation; unlinked fees, rebates, corporate actions, assignments and historical coverage still need review.
- Options state and compressed observations commit together in SQLite. The outbox exports immutable numbered gzip files outside the trading coordinator, verifies existing files after a crash and retains a hash chain. The 5 GiB journal quota fails closed. There is no automatic deletion of financial observations. Back up/export before migrating retention storage.
- Options primary replay verifies frame, state and record hashes. `stress-replay` evolves entries/exits/allocations independently at 3.5 seconds with adverse costs; it requires a flat experiment checkpoint. Dashboard “stress costs” remains only a same-path cost sensitivity. Neither is broker-fill evidence.
- Effective-dated listed-equity research fees replace the expired date cutoff. Prospective dates use the latest published schedule as an explicit assumption. A table change requires a documented `amend-fees` registration and full primary/stress repricing; signal rules and frozen dates cannot change through that amendment.
- Worker failures get at most five exponential-backoff restarts per process. Successful assessments are retained when another worker fails. An exhausted enabled worker remains visible and blocks readiness until repaired or disabled.
- Authenticated `/api/readiness`, `/api/incidents`, `/api/accounting` and dashboard panels separate entry gates, protection, incidents and economics. `/healthz` is liveness only. Runtime status includes a source digest and verified build manifest.
- Automatic local SQLite backups include the options outbox, undergo SQLite/hash-chain checks, and have SHA-256 manifests. `BACKUP_EXPORT_DIR` optionally exports them; the path alone does not prove off-host protection. The offline restore drill never touches the active journal.
- A local account writer socket prevents two engine processes using the same account/mode in the same network namespace, regardless of their data directories. Distinct Docker namespaces/hosts still require an operational single-writer deployment or distributed account lock.

## Commands

Run `npm run check` and `npm test` before deployment. `node scripts/recovery.js drill <new-directory>` creates, backs up, restores and re-exports a disposable fixture including a $2,000 override. `node scripts/recovery.js verify <backup.sqlite>` verifies its manifest. `export-options <backup.sqlite> <new-directory>` recovers compressed observations without mutating the source.

`node scripts/options-research.js replay <tape.jsonl-or-directory>` and `stress-replay <tape.jsonl-or-directory>` accept legacy JSONL and numbered gzip exports with a starting checkpoint. Preserve the matching source version: incompatible experiments must not be silently replayed under new rules.

When the stopped options ledger has **no open or pending positions**, `node scripts/recovery.js new-options-experiment <database.sqlite>` archives its prior state and starts a disabled experiment. It acquires the DB lease and refuses active exposure. Do not reset a ledger to conceal losses or evade a halt.

`node scripts/momentum-validation.js amend-fees registration.json justification.json amended.json` requires a reason and HTTPS source URL. It preserves the original registration inside the amended artifact. Both replay reports must use the amended fee table; the assessor still rejects changed signal code or dates.

`npm run replay:engine -- tape.json calendar.json result.json [settings.json]` is a coordinator-parity equity replay. Tape: `{schema:1,source:"recorded"|"synthetic",quoteSizeUnits:"shares",events:[...]}`. Events require monotonic receipt `now`, exchange `ts`, a `symbol`, and `kind:"quote"|"bar"|"heartbeat"`. Quotes require bid/ask and displayed sizes in shares for fills. Bars use the production bar schema and must be complete at receipt. `warmup:true` seeds context without trading. Calendar: `{observedAt,coverageFrom,coverageTo,sessions:[{date,open,close}]}` with epoch-millisecond times and real exchange early closes. Quotes, bars and heartbeats drive the real Engine, shared risk allocation and exits with a conservative delayed, size-capped simulated broker. Incomplete exposures are retained. Native bracket routing, repeated displayed liquidity, Jev effects, crypto context and actual broker execution still need separate calibration. Legacy `backtest` reports cannot qualify a deployment.

## Deployment and recovery

`powershell -File scripts/deploy-paper.ps1` builds and tests first, retains a rollback image, pauses new paper entries, creates and verifies an online backup, recreates only the engine service on 8080, and checks version, protection, source manifest and the $2,000 limit. It resumes only if previously unpaused and the account gates are clear. Failures leave entries paused. It never deletes the Docker volume. The script requires the Windows session to have working Docker access; it cannot fix ACLs. Its PowerShell syntax can be checked without executing deployment.

Before a manual restore, stop all writers for the account, inspect owned and external positions/orders at the broker, retain the current DB/tape/image, verify a backup, and restore to an isolated location first. A backup contains a copied lease and can be stale relative to broker fills. Clear a copied lease only in an explicitly chosen stopped restore; restart paused and reconcile broker identity, fills, signed quantities, settings and incidents before resuming. Never use a second empty journal against the same paper account as a deployment shortcut.

## Still required before the audit can be fully closed

1. Deploy and perform paper execution calibration on the actual environment. Local tests and synthetic replay do not demonstrate provider behavior under real faults.
2. Select/test an external dead-man alert destination and off-host backup destination. `HEARTBEAT_URL` and `BACKUP_EXPORT_DIR` remain unset unless supplied. Test alert delivery and recovery from loss of the host.
3. Implement and validate an options multi-leg broker lifecycle: partial fills, rejects, cancel/replace, assignment, exercise, expiration, corporate actions and underlying exposure. The options adapter remains read-only; no paper/live options orders are connected by this release.
4. Acquire/verify point-in-time float and security classifications, news receipt/classification, corporate-action bases, tick/lot rules, continuous halt status and full 04:00 coverage for small-cap research. Alpaca-only missing values remain exclusions, not guesses.
5. Collect complete executable-price tapes and unseen sessions; register a frozen portfolio experiment; include all attempted variants, operational costs and adverse execution; perform independent statistical and execution review. Options still lacks a completed preregistered statistical qualification workflow. No historical loss is erased and no strategy is called consistently profitable.
6. Complete statement-level cash/fee/corporate-action reconciliation and reviewed incident recovery, distributed writer fencing if multiple hosts are introduced, and an archival retention workflow beyond the local quota.

Fee convention source: [Robinhood trading fees](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/trading-fees-on-robinhood/). Options lifecycle requirements: [Alpaca options trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading) and [Level 3 multi-leg orders](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-level-3-trading). These implementation boundaries do not imply that commission-free execution is cost-free.
