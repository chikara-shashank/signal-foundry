# Research round 2 — frozen before inspecting new returns

Recorded 2026-09-25. Previous intraday studies failed net-cost validation. This round does not tune those parameters or search for the best ticker.

Equity universe fixed in advance: SPY, QQQ, IWM (large-cap, technology-heavy and small-cap US equity ETFs). Two hypotheses: prior completed month close above its 10-month simple average; positive prior 12-month return. Long/cash only; no leverage, shorts, volatility scaling or parameter search. Compare each with buy-and-hold on the identical dates. These are adaptations of Faber (2007/2013) and Moskowitz/Ooi/Pedersen (2012), not replications of their diversified portfolios.

Data: Alpaca SIP daily bars, 2014-01-01 through 2026-09-24. Adjusted bars for economic total-return proxies and monthly signals; raw prices for lot feasibility. Only completed months may influence a decision. Execute changes at the next available daily open. No interest on cash. Charge 4 basis points per side; stress at 12 basis points per side, and a separate one-session execution delay. Dividends/splits embedded in adjusted bars are a return proxy, not a broker cash/corporate-action ledger. Report data gaps and hashes.

Windows: 2016–2023 development; 2024–2025 retrospective validation; 2026 retrospective final period. These dates are already known to the research process and are not pristine holdouts. Report all candidates and windows, including losers; no winner selection followed by relabeling as out-of-sample. Also report fixed 9.5% engine-allocation sleeve returns. These normalized returns do not establish executable lot sizing or the performance of intraday paper-engine brackets. A separate capital-feasibility view uses the existing $500 position / $10 stop-risk constraints with a 2% protective stop.

Integration: add the 10-month model, chosen for transparency before results, as a default-off paper/shadow strategy. Keep shared overnight allocation below 10%, owned GTC protection, current per-trade and per-symbol limits, external ownership exclusions, and 15:30–15:55 New York entry window. A 20-session maximum holding deadline, 2% protective stop and 6% target are engine adaptations, not the published model. Do not certify the handler from the normalized historical study.

Options hypothesis: monthly OTM iron condor motivated by Cboe CNDR and published volatility-risk-premium research. SPY/QQQ American ETF option adaptation: same expiry 21–45 DTE, short deltas 0.15–0.25, narrower 1–5 dollar protective wings, both short IVs at least 1.25 times prior realized volatility. Portfolio accounting must treat all four legs as one position and reserve maximum wing loss less net credit, including costs. $100 maximum risk / $200 aggregate risk, default off, quote-shadow only. Existing profit/time/loss exits remain explicit deviations from CNDR settlement/roll rules.

Analyze official CNDR index history alongside PUT/BXM, with no claim it reproduces our contracts or costs. Our executable options backtest remains blocked until historical synchronized bid/ask and point-in-time chain metadata exist. Extend the recorder/replay to four legs; test adverse latency/costs independently. Never substitute option candles, modeled prices or the published index for executable strategy returns.

No new strategy is live-qualified by this round. Actual forward validation starts no earlier than 2026-09-28 under new source fingerprints. No active-service restart, broker orders, risk-limit increases or automatic strategy enablement.

Data-availability amendment before any return calculation: Alpaca returned SPY daily history beginning 2016-01-04, with 504 pre-2016 calendar sessions missing from the requested window. The loader rejected the incomplete dataset. Use 2016-01-01 as the data start and 2017-02-01 as the common performance start, allowing 13 completed months for the 12-month comparison. Development and full windows therefore begin February 2017. Do not invent earlier prices.

Funded adaptation experiment frozen after normalized signal results, before inspecting funded returns: obtain raw SIP five-minute bars for the same three ETFs and dates. Simulate a combined $10,000 managed book, $350 per-position overnight ceiling (3.5%), $950 shared overnight ceiling (9.5%), $500 ordinary position limit, $10 stop-risk budget, integer shares, 2% stop / 6% target, one filled entry per symbol/month, 20-session deadline at 15:55. First entry opportunity 15:30; fixed SPY/QQQ/IWM ordering for simultaneous eligibility. Stops fill at worse of gap-open or stop, stop first if both bounds touched; target fills no better than target. Base 4bps/side, stress 12bps/side, separate one-five-minute-bar delay. Pending external holdings/other strategies are absent, making available capital optimistic; data are trade bars, not historical quotes. A single simulated sleeve is not the whole engine. End-of-study open trades are explicitly liquidated for comparison. These are retrospective diagnostics, not a promotion criterion. No new parameter search.
